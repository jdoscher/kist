# Kist brand

Kist is a self-hosted visual library for people and their AI agents.

**Tagline:** Keep what catches your eye.

Every asset here is generated by `docs/brand/build_brand.py` from one
construction, so a change to the mark is one edit plus a re-run
(`python docs/brand/build_brand.py --raster`, needs fontTools and Pillow;
see the script header). Masters live in `docs/brand/`, the shipped icons in
`web/public/`, and the inline paths the app uses in `web/src/brandPaths.ts`.

## The mark: Brass corners

Two L-shaped brass brackets at opposite corners of an implied square, and a
small square in the middle: the kept item. It reads three ways at once: the
brass corner guards on a wooden chest, the paper corners that hold a photo in
an album, and a designer's crop marks. Chest, photographs, picked.

![mark](brand/kist.svg)

### Construction (64-unit grid)

Every length is a multiple of 4, so at 16 px (4 units per pixel) every edge
lands on a whole pixel.

| Part | Bare mark (`GLYPH`) | On an app tile (`APP`) | On a small tile (`TAB`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frame | 4–60 | 12–52 | 8–56 |
| Bracket arm length | 24 | 20 | 20 |
| Bracket thickness | 8 | 8 | 8 |
| Kept item | 8 × 8, centred | 8 × 8 | 8 × 8 |
| Used for | favicon.svg, mono, dashboards, in-app, lockup | apple-touch, 192/512, maskable | favicon.ico, favicon-32.png |

- Outer corner of each bracket: radius 2. Inner corner and the arm ends:
  radius 0.5, so it looks stamped from metal rather than drawn as a line icon.
  Kept item: radius 1.5.
- The two brackets never overlap or run past each other (that is what keeps
  it apart from Lucide's `crop`).
- At 16 px the mark is two 2 px-thick Ls and a 2 px dot: ⌜ · ⌟.
- **Maskable:** full-bleed tile, the APP mark scaled to 0.8 about the centre.
  Its corners sit 22.6 units from the centre, inside the 25.6-unit (40 %)
  safe circle.
- **Monochrome:** the mark is solid shapes only, so it survives iOS tinting
  and single-colour use without change. `icon-mono.svg` (black, manifest
  `monochrome`), `kist-light.svg` (white, for dark dashboards),
  `kist-dark.svg` (#1C1B19, for light dashboards).
- **Clear space:** at least the bracket thickness (8 units = 1/7 of the bare
  mark) on every side; in the lockup, the gap between mark and wordmark.
- **Minimum size:** 16 px for the bare mark; the tile versions from 16 px.

## Wordmark

Lowercase **kist** in Instrument Serif Regular, tracking −1 %, outlined to
paths (no font needed at runtime). The dot of the i is a small brass square
that echoes the kept item in the mark (it replaces the round dot, centred
where it was, 92 % of its width).

- Lockup: mark height = cap height × 1.25, centred on the cap height; gap =
  twice the i's stem width. (`lockup-dark.svg`, `lockup-light.svg`)
- Ink: #ECEAE4 on dark, #1C1B19 on light. Dot: #E8B04B on dark, #8C5A00 on
  light.
- Never set the name in capitals in the wordmark; in running text it is
  "Kist".

- **Gallery grey** is for judging colour: one neutral mid-grey (#5C5C5C)
  behind everything, top bar, side panels, grid and full-screen preview
  alike, so the images are the only colour on screen. A thin light pinstripe
  (`chrome-line`) marks where the chrome meets the images. Two greys side by
  side read as a mistake, so the chrome never steps away from the canvas;
  menus and dialogs, which float, step darker (`--bg-float`). (Until Nov 2026 the canvas was
  the 18 % grey #767676, but on that grey only pure white text passes AA, so
  one surface there would have cost the brass links and coloured error text.)
- **Light theme: neutral canvas, grey preview.** The chrome (top bar,
  sidebar, panels) stays warm paper, but the image canvas is a neutral
  #ECECEC: a warm near-white surround makes photos look cooler and darker
  than they are. The full-screen preview in light is the gallery grey
  #5C5C5C with gallery's preview colours, not near-black: jumping from
  paper to #0D0D0C was hard on the eye, and the mid-grey is the neutral
  surround photo apps offer for judging. This reverses the earlier "the
  preview always stays dark" rule; Jay approved it in the Nov 2026
  aesthetic pass. Dark keeps its near-black preview.
- **Preview tokens.** The preview and compare/survey overlays take their
  colours from `--preview-*` in themes.css (text, dim, accent, buttons,
  notes panel, field line, danger), contrast-checked like everything else,
  instead of hard-coded overrides. In gallery and light `preview-dim` is
  #dadada, a visible step below the white text (the caption's extension
  and dimensions).
- **Preview layout (desktop).** Nothing sits on the picture: 64 px bands
  above and below the image (`--preview-band`; the image gets
  `--preview-room`, 100vh − 128 px) hold the controls. Top right: Loupe,
  Notes and Close as matching 32 px ghost buttons in one row
  (`.preview-band`). Bottom: a one-line Inter 13 caption,
  "name.ext · 3840 × 2628 px · 1 of 41" with the extension and facts in
  `preview-dim`, then "More like this" and "Open original" as underlined
  text actions. Both arrows look the same at rest (`--text` at 70 %);
  the one with nowhere to go is hidden. The phone keeps its own layout.
- **Browser and launch colours.** The `theme-color` meta (the phone's
  status bar) is each theme's `chrome` (`THEME_COLOR` in `theme.ts`), and
  `public/theme-init.js` paints `<html>` in the theme's `bg` before the
  stylesheet loads (`THEME_BG`), so gallery and light never flash the dark
  #131312. The manifest's splash colour can only follow the OS scheme, not
  the stored theme. `tests/theme.test.ts` checks all three against themes.css.
- Never use the bright brass #E8B04B as a fill or text on a light
  background: it is 1.86:1 there. Use #8C5A00.

### Brass budget

Brass means one of four things, and nothing else:

1. **the primary action** (`.btn-primary` / `button.primary`: Upload, Save,
   New token; one per view);
2. **selection on images**: the tile corners and check (the theme's
   `--canvas-focus`, never a hard-coded #E8B04B);
3. **the focus ring**;
4. **ratings** (stars).

Everything else is neutral: tag, folder and board chips use `--chip-bg` /
`--chip-text` (tags are pills, folders have 6 px corners), inspector links
are `--text` with a thin underline, and a pressed toggle is a fill, not a
second brass button. Brass on everything means nothing.

A selected row, section, chip or pressed toggle (sidebar, Settings nav, ⌘K
active row, tag chips, the Info toggle, tile-shape and board tools) is a
neutral fill (`--accent-soft` / `--bg-active`, both neutral greys despite the
name) with a 1 px `--selected-line` outline that follows the control's own
corners. No brass bars on a side or underneath: Jay found they read like a
bracket hanging off the control (Nov 2026). Native checkboxes, radios, sliders and progress bars use `accent-color: var(--text)`, and the date scrubber's position mark is `--canvas-text`, so neither adds brass.

## Shared primitives (for anyone building UI)

Use these instead of new one-off values; each is documented below.

| Need | Use |
|---|---|
| A bar or side panel next to the images | `background: var(--chrome)`, edge `1px solid var(--chrome-line)` (never `--bg-raised` / `--border`; `tests/chrome.test.ts`) |
| A button | plain `<button>` (quiet), `.btn-primary` (one per view), `.icon-btn` (28 × 28 icon-only) |
| A pressed toggle | `.on` (fill; `.btn-icon` / `.icon-btn` / `.seg` add the 1 px `--selected-line` outline) |
| A field or select | `<input>`, `<select>` (own chevron), `<textarea>` |
| A selected row in a list | fill + `box-shadow: inset 3px 0 0 var(--accent)` |
| A chip (tag, folder, board) | `.chip` (`--chip-bg` / `--chip-text`), `.chip.folder-chip` for square-ish corners |
| Something that floats | `.float-surface` (or `--bg-float`, `--float-line`, `--float-shadow`, `--float-radius` / `--dialog-radius`) |
| Appearing / leaving | `--dur-in` + `--ease-out` with `@keyframes float-in` / `float-up` / `panel-in`; exit with `fade-out` over `--dur-out` (`usePresence` in `motion.ts`) |
| Phone sheet or drawer motion | `--dur-sheet` + `--ease-sheet` |
| A view title / folder page title | `.view-title` (24 px serif) / `.page-title` (34 px) + `.title-eyebrow`, count in `.title-count` |
| A small section label | `.section-label` |
| A machine string | `.mono` |
| Tile corners | `--tile-radius` (4 / compact 0), `--board-radius` (2) |
| The preview surround | `--preview-*` tokens |

## Controls

One height, three kinds. Nothing else.

| Kind | Class | At rest | Hover | Pressed / on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet | any plain `<button>` (incl. `.icon-btn`, `.btn-icon`) | no fill, no border | `--bg-hover` fill | `--bg-active` fill + 1 px `--selected-line` outline (`.on`) |
| Primary | `.btn-primary` | `--accent` fill, `--on-accent` label | `--accent-hover` | |
| Field | `input`, `select`, `textarea` | `--field-border` outline on `--bg` | | `--accent` border on focus |

- Every control is `--ctl-h` 28 px tall with `--ctl-radius` 6 px corners
  and 8 px side padding (`--ctl-pad-x`); icon-only quiet buttons are
  exactly 28 × 28. Phone layouts keep 44 px hit areas with their own
  min-height or an `::after` (phone.css), not by changing the look.
- Fields need the 3:1 outline (1.4.11); quiet buttons don't, because their
  label or icon identifies them. Keep a label on anything that isn't a
  well-known glyph.
- Selects are fields with Kist's own chevron (`--chevron` in themes.css,
  the theme's dim text colour), `appearance: none`, still real `<select>`s.
- One `.btn-primary` per view (Upload, Save, New token). `button.danger` is
  a quiet button with `--danger` text.
- Links inside a sentence (`.linkish`, `.link-btn`, `.btn-link`,
  `.link-like`) are underlined `--text`, sized like text, not 28 px tall.

### Top bar and grid header (desktop)

- The top bar is one 48 px line: brand, the search box (32 px, one line,
  starting over the grid's left edge, the palette's `⌘K` / `Ctrl K` as a
  keycap inside it that opens the palette), a spacer, Paste and Upload (the
  only brass control), a `--chrome-line` hairline (`.topbar-sep`), then the
  quiet icons (Info, Recent changes, Share, Appearance, Settings). Filter
  chips stay on the line and scroll sideways.
- The view controls (tile shape, size, type, filters, rating, sort) sit in
  the grid header (`.grid-meta`) on the right, next to what they change;
  the title (`.view-title`, or `.page-title` with a `.title-eyebrow` on a
  folder or tag page) and its count on the left, on the tiles' left edge.
  Narrow grids (container query) drop the Size and Filters words. The
  header stays while a new list loads, so a control keeps focus.
- The phone keeps its own two-row bar (`ViewControls mobile` in the top
  bar).
- The selection bar (`.bulkbar`) shows only with two or more selected (one
  selected: the info panel has every action). It is one 40 px row docked
  under the grid, in the flow, on `--chrome` with a `--chrome-line` rule
  above: no float, no shadow. Order: count, clear, Tag field, Folder,
  Board, Pick, Reject, Label, then More (everything else, opening upward)
  and Trash last in `--danger`.

### Settings and Appearance layout

- Settings content is one 640 px column (`--settings-col`); a section's
  head puts its buttons (New token, Check again) on that column's right
  edge, not the window's.
- The Appearance theme options show each theme's image canvas as a 20 × 14
  colour chip (System: half light, half dark), and radios and chips sit on
  the first line of a two-line option.

### Boards

- The board bar is one 48 px row on `--chrome` that never wraps: back,
  the board's name as the view title (Instrument Serif 24, sized to its
  text), save state, then tool groups split by a `--chrome-line`
  hairline, and on the right zoom, List, background, Export and Share.
  The active tool is the pressed fill plus the thin `--selected-line` outline. Below
  about 1240 px the text labels (Boards, Library, List, Export) fold to
  icons and stay in the accessible name. The phone has its own header.
- Items on a board are prints on a table: `--board-radius` (2 px), a
  contact shadow plus a soft lift
  (`0 1px 2px #0006, 0 14px 30px -10px #000a`); below 30 % zoom only the
  contact shadow stays.
- Board background "Theme" (`canvas.bg: "theme"` in board.json) is the
  default for new boards: the board sits on the theme's image canvas, and
  text and arrows without their own colour use `--canvas-text` /
  `--canvas-dim`. Boards from before the design pass keep their colour.
  Exports and the board thumbnail have no theme, so a theme board renders
  on the gallery grey #5c5c5c with white default ink.

| Dialogs and the command palette | 12 px | `--dialog-radius` |

## Grid tiles

The images lead; anything drawn on them earns its place.

- **Loading:** a tile with no ThumbHash or colour yet is `--tile-wait`
  (one step off `--canvas`: gallery #676767, dark #232321, light #E0E0E0;
  decorative, no contrast minimum) with a faint 1.4 s sheen (none under
  reduced motion). Badges stay hidden until the thumbnail has loaded
  (`img.loaded`), or failed (`img.failed`, which also stops the sheen).
- **Ratings (quiet tiles):** one brass star and a tabular number ("★ 5"),
  bottom right on the caption's dark gradient, only on hover, keyboard
  focus or selection; on every tile while the grid is filtered or sorted
  by rating (`.ratings-shown`, `ratingsShown()` in query.ts). Never on a
  phone tile. The rating is always in the tile's `aria-label`.
- **Stacks:** a closed stack's entry (`.tile.stacked`) has a second sheet
  4 px up and right behind it (`--tile-wait` fill, `--field-border` edge,
  3:1 on the canvas), drawn with box shadows. Its count badge shows on
  hover, focus and selection; compact density has no room for the sheet
  and keeps the badge. The count is in the aria-label ("stack of 2 items").

- **Gutters and the date rail:** 12 px from the sidebar to the first tile
  and 12 px from the last tile to the 30 px date rail; the rail starts
  8 px below the grid header, year labels are centred in it and hang 4 px
  below their tick (clear of the brass mark and the info panel's line).
- **Counts:** the grid header leads with the same number as the sidebar
  (every item) and adds the tiles when closed stacks make fewer: "43 items
  · 41 tiles" (`gridCountText` / `sidebarCount` in query.ts). A search or
  filter shows just its number of results.

- **Empty states** (`EmptyState` in GridExtras.tsx): the outline mark
  (`OutlineMark`, Brand.tsx: brackets as thin `--canvas-dim` lines, the
  kept item a brass square; `aria-hidden`), one Instrument Serif line
  (24 px, at most 22ch), one dim 13 px line and at most one action. Empty
  Unfiled, Trash, Picks, Rejects, a folder, a tag, a smart folder and a
  search with no hits all use it (words in `emptyState`, query.ts); the
  view's subtitle hides while the grid is empty so nothing is said twice.

## Sidebar

- **Labels** get their own section head under Cull: 12 px colour dots
  with a 1 px `--field-border` ring (the ring carries the 3:1 boundary,
  since some label colours alone don't reach it on the grey), each in a
  24 px target, the first on the icon column; pressed adds a `--text`
  ring. The info panel's and triage's label pickers stay 24 px discs
  (editing controls).
- **Tags** show as a wrapped cloud of 24 px neutral chips (`.tag-cloud-chip`:
  `--chip-bg` / `--chip-text`, count in `--text-dim`, no `#` icon); a tag
  you're filtering by takes the selected fill (`--accent-soft` with a 1 px
  `--selected-line` edge). Two small toggles in the Tags head switch to the list
  (better for hundreds of tags); the choice is a pref (`kist.tagView`).

## Type

| Role | Face | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Title (`.view-title`) | Instrument Serif 400, 24 px (`--fs-5`), line-height 1.05, −0.005em | every view, page and panel title: Timeline, Map, Boards, Triage, each Settings and Organise section, "No selection" |
| Page title (`.page-title`) | Instrument Serif 400, 34 px (`--fs-6`) | a folder or tag page, with a `.title-eyebrow` (Inter 11 px, dim) naming the parent folder above it |
| UI | Inter Variable | everything below a title |
| Counts (`.title-count`, `.count`) | Inter, `tabular-nums` | numbers a person reads: counts, sizes, dates, "1 of 41" |
| Data (`.mono`) | JetBrains Mono Variable, 11 px | strings a machine made: hash, MIME type, URL, pixel dimensions, hex, version |
| Wordmark, empty states, crash page | Instrument Serif | sparingly |

Instrument Serif is a display face: never below 18 px, never bold, never
for UI labels. On the image canvas a title uses `--canvas-text` and its
count `--canvas-dim` (the grid header).

All three are OFL and self-hosted through Fontsource (no CDN). Inter's
italic is loaded as its own file (`wght-italic.css`), and `body` sets
`font-synthesis: none`, so the browser never fakes an italic or a bold:
Instrument Serif exists only at 400, so never set it bold. Scale (six
steps): 11 / 13 / 15 / 18 / 24 / 34 px (`--fs-1` … `--fs-6`); 13 px is the
body size. Counts and sizes use tabular figures.

- **Every** `font-size` in the stylesheets is `var(--fs-N)` (or `inherit`).
  `web/tests/type-scale.test.ts` fails on any raw size; a genuine exception
  carries `/* type-scale: allow (why) */` on the same line. The allowed ones
  today: 16 px phone fields (iOS Safari zooms into anything smaller), the
  28 px default of a board text node (board content, the user's own scale)
  and the font-specimen glyphs (the item's own font).
- `h1`–`h6` are reset (no browser margins; 15 px / 600, h3–h6 13 px / 600),
  so a heading never picks up the browser's 1.5em/1.17em sizes.
- `.mono` is 11 px.
- **Prose** (paragraphs, hints, notes, descriptions, view subtitles):
  line-height 1.5, at most 64 characters a line (`max-width: 64ch`).
- **Small labels and section heads** (`.section-label`, sidebar heads,
  palette groups, Appearance legends): 11 px / 600, uppercase,
  `letter-spacing: .08em`, `font-feature-settings: 'case'`, `--text-dim`.
  Caps only for these labels, never inside content.
- **Sentence case** for every UI string: "Triage unfiled", "In trash",
  "Go to all items". The copy carries the case; no `text-transform:
  capitalize`. A view's name used as a name stays as written ("Nothing in
  Unfiled").
- **Secondary text is a visible step dimmer**: `--text-dim` / `--canvas-dim`
  is #DADADA in gallery (4.78:1 on the grey, against 6.13:1 for `--text`);
  dark and light already step from ~15–16:1 down to ~6:1.

Lucide (ISC), imported per icon. Icon-only buttons keep a visible target of

## Motion

| Token | Value | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| --dur-1 | 120 ms | hover, press, captions |
| --dur-2 | 200 ms | panels, sheets, drawer, thumbnails fading in |
| --dur-3 | 280 ms | the grid tile growing into the preview |
| --dur-in | 120 ms | something appearing: menus, popovers, dialogs, toasts, the info panel |
| --dur-out | 80 ms | something leaving (faster than it came) |
| --dur-sheet | 380 ms | phone sheet and drawer (with `--ease-sheet`) |

Easings: `--ease` `cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1)` for state changes,
`--ease-out` `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)` for things appearing, and
`--ease-sheet` `cubic-bezier(.32, .72, 0, 1)` (the iOS sheet curve) for the
phone sheet and drawer only.

- On a phone the item sheet slides up, the drawer slides in, its backdrop
  fades, and the Move to… / Tags bottom sheets rise with their scrim, all
  over `--dur-sheet` 380 ms on `--ease-sheet` (phone.css; the sheet's
  drag-release snaps back on the same curve). Desktop never uses these
  tokens (`tests/motion.test.ts`).

- Menus, popovers, dialogs and the palette fade in and slide 4 px down
  (`@keyframes float-in`); toasts and notices rise 4 px (`float-up`).
  Keep it under 150 ms: no bounce, no scale-up.
- The info panel slides in 8 px from the right (`panel-in`) and fades out
  over `--dur-out` before it unmounts (`usePresence` in `src/motion.ts`,
  whose `DUR_IN_MS` / `DUR_OUT_MS` match the CSS; a test checks).
- Buttons give way on press: `scale(.98)`.
- Opening and closing the preview is a shared-element View Transition when
  the browser supports it.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every duration token drops to
  ~0, every animation and transition is cut, the press scale is off, the
  info panel closes at once and no view transition runs. (Kist has no
  in-app motion setting; the OS setting is the switch.)

## Floating surfaces

Anything that floats over the page (menus, popovers, dialogs, the ⌘K
palette, toasts, notices) uses one recipe, so it reads as one family:

- surface `--bg-float` (dark #21211F, light #FFFFFF, gallery #454545: a
  clear step darker than the page, which the old `--bg-raised` #565656
  wasn't), with `--float-hover` for hovered rows inside it;
- a hairline `--float-line` border;
- `--float-shadow`: an inner top highlight plus a far and a near shadow
  (`--float-sheen`, `--float-shadow-far`, `--float-shadow-near`);
- `--float-radius` 10 px (menus, popovers, toasts) or `--dialog-radius`
  12 px (dialogs, palette);
- behind dialogs a lighter 40 % `--scrim`, so the images don't go muddy.

New floating things use the `.float-surface` class (styles.css), which
also brings the `float-in` animation. The palette's footer shows its keys
as keycaps (`<kbd>`).

## Phone

The phone is its own layout (`.app-shell.mobile`, rules in `phone.css`),
built to sit next to Photos on a home screen rather than look like the
desktop shrunk down.

- **Top bar.** Row one: a 44 px borderless menu button, the mark, and a
  44 px pill search field sunk into the chrome (`--phone-sunken`, 22 px
  radius). No ⌘K button (a phone has no ⌘ key) and no Export button: Share
  and Export sit in the drawer head. Row two: every control (tile shape,
  − n +, Type, Filters, Rating, Sort) has one borderless fill
  (`--phone-fill`, 10 px radius); widths stay as `fitSelect.ts PHONE_ROW`
  measures them.
- **Large title.** The view title is Instrument Serif 34 px (`--fs-6`) on
  the canvas, its count Inter 11 px tabular.
- **Item sheet.** `--bg-raised` with the `--chrome-line` pinstripe on its
  top edge, 20 px top corners and a long soft shadow, so it lifts off the
  grid; a visible 36 × 5 grabber (`--field-border`); neutral chips;
  Move to… fills the action row and Trash is a 52 px icon button in
  `--danger` (its accessible name stays "Trash"). The tile that is open in
  the sheet shows the brass corners only, no multi-select check.
- **Drawer head.** Mark and wordmark on the left, Share/Export and a 44 px
  borderless ✕ on the right, on `--chrome` with the pinstripe below.
- **Preview capsules.** Everything over the full-screen image (Notes,
  close, the arrows, the stars, Tags / Move / Trash) floats in one glass
  surface: `--preview-capsule` at 72 % with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)
  saturate(1.4)`, a 16 % hairline and a soft shadow. Under
  `prefers-reduced-transparency` or `prefers-contrast: more` it is the solid
  `--preview-capsule`. Stars sit in their own 24 px-radius capsule above a
  30 px-radius bar of 64 × 54 buttons; the arrows are 48 px circles on the
  bar's line. Stars there use `--preview-star` (light's #8C5A00 brass is
  too dark on the capsule). Blur is kept to these few elements.
- **Board header.** One 57 px row: a 44 px back button, the board name in
  Instrument Serif 24 px, "Saved" 11 px dim, and a 44 px ••• menu
  (`PhoneBoardMenu.tsx`) holding List, Export (PNG, PDF, HTML) and Share.
  The zoom (−, %, +, fit) floats in a capsule at the bottom centre on the
  `--bg-float` glass (solid under reduced transparency), with 48 × 44
  buttons.
- **44 px hit areas (A8).** Small phone controls keep their look and get a
  transparent `::after` that grows the tap target: −/+ and the other
  row-two controls to 44 px tall (−/+ share the count between them: about
  36 × 44, the row has no more width), and in the item sheet the close
  button, View, the colour swatches, More like this, Edit and History to
  about 44 px tall (40–44 where two neighbours' areas meet; neither steals
  the other's tap). The list in phone.css uses `:where()` so each
  control's own inset wins.

## Voice

- Calm and concrete. Name the file, the folder, the count.
- Verbs: keep, find, file, pick.
- No exclamation marks.
- Errors say what happened, what's safe, and what to do next.
- **The name.** In Scots and Dutch "kist" can also mean a coffin. The main
  sense everywhere is a chest you keep treasures in, and nothing in the
  brand should pull the other way:
  - never use "kist" as a verb;
  - never write hoard, stash, bury, rest, laid, dead or deceased;
  - no tapered or hexagonal box shapes, no black lidded box;
  - pair the name with the descriptor where people meet it first: "Kist, a
    self-hosted visual library".
